# Community code of conduct

MC Poser welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels.
These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and
project community discussions.

## Be constructive

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent.
- Give specific, helpful feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respect differences of opinion and accept maintainer decisions.
- Be patient with new contributors and people asking for help.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, spam, and
publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

## Report a concern

Contact **PockyDev** privately through [Pockyverse](https://discord.pcky.dev).
If direct messages are unavailable, ask for a private contact route without
posting sensitive details publicly. Include only the information needed to
understand the concern. Reports should be handled with discretion.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, and
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Responses depend
on the nature and severity of the behavior.
