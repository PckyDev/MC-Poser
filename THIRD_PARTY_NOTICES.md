third-party software, skins, artwork, or trademarks. Those materials retain
their own terms. A code license does not grant trademark rights.

## Software

MC Poser uses React, Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, skinview3d, Font Awesome, and
other packages listed in `package.json` and `package-lock.json`. Their license
notices are distributed with their respective packages. Preserve applicable
notices when redistributing those packages or a build that includes them.

use. Exporting an image, sharing a workspace, or downloading diagnostics does
